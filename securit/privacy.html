Securit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Securit, developed by ITS Security, handles information when you use the Securit Android application.

Securit is designed to help users review visible applications installed on their Android device and identify apps that may deserve a closer look.

1. Information Securit Accesses

Securit may access information about applications that are visible to the app through Android's application and package-management system.

This may include information such as:

  • Application names
  • Application package names
  • The number of visible applications on the device

This information is used on the device to perform Securit's app-review functionality and calculate scan results.

2. Installed Application Information

Securit reviews visible application names against review criteria to identify apps that may warrant additional attention.

Securit does not upload or transmit your installed-app list to an ITS Security server.

A suggested app does not necessarily indicate that the application is malicious, unsafe, or infected with malware.

3. Scan History

If scan history is available through your version of Securit, information about previous scans may be saved locally on your device.

Scan-history information may include:

  • Security score
  • Number of apps suggested for review
  • Date of the scan

Scan history is stored locally on your device and can be removed using Securit's Clear Scan History feature.

4. App Settings

Certain Securit preferences, including settings such as weekly scan reminders, may be stored locally on your device.

ITS Security does not use these locally stored preferences to build advertising profiles or sell information about you.

5. Notifications

Securit may request permission to send notifications.

If enabled, notifications may be used for features such as weekly security-scan reminders.

Notification permission can be disabled at any time through your Android device settings.

6. Personal Information

Securit does not require users to create a Securit account or provide personal information such as a name, mailing address, telephone number, or date of birth in order to use the app.

7. Financial Information

ITS Security does not directly collect or store your credit card, debit card, or banking information through the Securit application.

If you purchase a Securit Premium subscription, payment and subscription processing is handled through Google Play. Payment information handled by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

8. Health Information

Securit does not provide health or medical functionality and does not intentionally collect health or medical information.

9. Advertising

Securit does not currently use third-party advertising networks within the app.

If advertising or promotional functionality is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

10. Data Sharing and Sale

ITS Security does not sell personal information collected through Securit.

Securit does not currently transmit scan-history data or installed-app review information to ITS Security for sale or advertising purposes.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Scan history and application preferences described in this policy are stored locally on the user's device.

Scan history can be deleted using the Clear Scan History feature within Securit's settings.

Removing Securit from the device may also remove locally stored application data according to Android's normal application-storage behavior.

12. Device Security Disclaimer

Securit is an informational and app-review tool. It does not perform a complete antivirus or malware scan and does not guarantee that a device is free from malware, viruses, spyware, or other security threats.

13. Children's Privacy

Securit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Securit does not require a user account or directly request personal identifying information, ITS Security does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the app.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ITS Security may update this Privacy Policy as Securit changes or as legal, technical, or regulatory requirements evolve.

When changes are made, the effective date displayed at the top of this page may be updated.
